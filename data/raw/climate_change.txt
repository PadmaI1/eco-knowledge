        Climate Change


Climate change refers to long-term changes in Earth's temperatures
and weather patterns. Human activities, particularly burning fossil fuels,
deforestation, and industrial processes, are major contributors to recent global warming.



Carbon dioxide (CO2) and methane are greenhouse gases that trap heat
in the atmosphere.     Increasing concentrations of these gases contribute
to rising global temperatures.


Major effects include melting glaciers, rising sea levels,


extreme weather events, droughts, and biodiversity loss.


    Climate change can be addressed through renewable energy,
energy efficiency, forest conservation, sustainable transportation,
and reducing greenhouse gas emissions.




Climate change affects both natural ecosystems and human communities.
