
Solar energy is a renewable energy source obtained from sunlight.

Solar panels convert sunlight into electricity using photovoltaic cells. This allows homes and businesses to generate electricity without directly burning fossil fuels.

Solar energy can reduce dependence on coal and natural gas. It can also reduce greenhouse gas emissions during electricity generation.

However, manufacturing solar panels requires raw materials and energy. Mining these materials can have environmental consequences.

Solar panel disposal and recycling are also important challenges. Recycling can recover valuable materials, but the process requires appropriate infrastructure.
