        Solar Energy


Solar energy is renewable energy obtained from sunlight.    Solar panels use
photovoltaic (PV) cells to convert sunlight into electricity.



Solar energy is clean during operation and reduces dependence on
fossil fuels such as coal and natural gas.


    The main advantages of solar energy are low operating costs,
reduced greenhouse gas emissions, and abundant availability.

However, electricity generation depends on sunlight, making
energy storage batteries important for nighttime use.



India has significant solar energy potential due to its geographical
location and high levels of sunlight throughout the year.
