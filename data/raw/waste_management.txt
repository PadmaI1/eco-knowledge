       Waste Management


Waste management involves collecting, processing, recycling, and disposing
of waste in an environmentally responsible way.



Waste can be classified into several categories, including biodegradable
waste, recyclable waste, hazardous waste, and electronic waste.


The 3Rs of waste management are Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle.


    Reducing waste at its source is generally more effective than
managing it after disposal.



Composting converts organic waste, such as food scraps and leaves,
into nutrient-rich material that improves soil quality.



Poor waste management can cause soil contamination, water pollution,
air pollution, and health problems.


        Proper segregation and recycling help reduce landfill waste
and conserve natural resources.




Electronic waste requires special handling because it may contain
hazardous substances such as lead and mercury.
